Hot Dog Survey Data

- Provenance:
  Around 200 opinions of UVA undergraduates, through an online survey, collected based on the question of "Is a hotdog a sandwich?."
  
- Data dictionary:
  Name, Undergraduate, Major, Answer, Timestamp

  The first three are general information of the UVA undergraduates, along with the answer of Yes or No of "Is a hotdog a sandwich". Timestamp is when the survey was filled out.
  
- Exploratory Plots:
  Pie chart for overall undergraduate opinion. Bar graphs split between majors.
  

- Quantification of Uncertainty:
  This survey is a small sample of UVA undergrads so result is going to be an estimate. 66.7% said No and 33.3% said Yes. This are likely to change based off of margin of error that is calculated: +-(%).
  
- Conclusions:
  It can be concluded that it is likely that around 66.7% of the UVA undergraduates believe that the hotdog is not a sandwich. The majority of the UVA undergraduates will say that a hotdog is not a sandwich.
